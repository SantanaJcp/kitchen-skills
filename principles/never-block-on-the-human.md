# Never Block on the Human

- **Parent:** `owner-attention` ([PRINCIPLES.md](../PRINCIPLES.md))
- **When:** you are tempted to ask "should I do X?" on reversible work.

The owner supervises afterwards, not in the loop. On reversible work, decide, do it and show the result; a wrong call on a two-way door costs less than a blocked agent.

- Proceed, then present: do X and say why, instead of asking whether to.
- When you notice a problem, record it and fix it in the next round.
- Product direction comes from the owner; execution does not wait for them.

Where the kitchen differs from pstack: this never covers one-way doors (`doors`), nor anything sent to people or published. Those wait for the owner's explicit request in the conversation, on every rung.
