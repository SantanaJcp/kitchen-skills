# Exhaust the Design Space

- **Parent:** `doors` ([PRINCIPLES.md](../PRINCIPLES.md))
- **When:** a one-way door, or a new interaction or architectural decision with no precedent in the codebase.

Build two or three competing designs and compare them side by side before you commit. Sketches or throwaway prototypes are enough; the point is to see the options before the choice is expensive.

- The options must differ in shape. A second flavor of the first shape does not count.
- It applies to product design too, where the experience depends on feel rather than logic: prototype it and look.
- For a one-way door, the owner picks between the options before anything runs.
